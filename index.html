Our Mission

Rescue, care, and rehome pets while spreading love and awareness. At Little Hooman, we believe every rescued pet carries a story — sometimes of abandonment, sometimes of neglect, but always of hope. We open our hearts to the voiceless, giving them warmth, safety, and a second chance at happiness. Until they find a forever home, they are not just animals in our care — they are family.
